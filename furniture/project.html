Kitchen in graphite and oak

Area12 m²Term5 weeksPrice420,000 ₽Year2025

The task

A family of four wanted a dark kitchen that does not look heavy, with a lot of storage and a place for breakfast.

The solution

Graphite enamel fronts on the bottom, oak veneer on top and open shelves to keep the room light. Drawers instead of doors in the bottom row, so everything is visible at once.

The result

The kitchen was assembled in one day. Storage grew by 40% compared to the old one, and the family has breakfast at the bar.
